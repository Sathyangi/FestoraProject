# Festora - Sri Lankan Cultural Heritage Platform

## Overview
Festora is a comprehensive full-stack web application dedicated to preserving and celebrating Sri Lanka's rich cultural and religious heritage. The platform serves as a digital hub connecting communities with traditional festivals, cultural products, and charitable initiatives across the island nation.

## Key Features

### Festival Management & Discovery
- **Comprehensive Festival Database:** Explore Buddhist, Hindu, Muslim, Christian, and multi-religious festivals with detailed information on rituals, schedules, and historical significance.  
- **Advanced Filtering System:** Filter festivals by religion, date, and location.  
- **Festival Details:** Access in-depth information including key rituals, significant locations, and typical schedules.  
- **Virtual Greeting Cards:** Create and share personalized, animated greeting cards for any festival with customizable templates and messages.

### E-Commerce Platform
- **Cultural Products Marketplace:** Browse and purchase 11+ categories of authentic Sri Lankan cultural items including adornments, carvings, ceremonial items, decorations, traditional food, musical instruments, religious items, and textiles.  
- **Smart Product Filtering:** Filter by category, availability status, and search functionality.  
- **Secure Shopping Cart:** Integrated shopping experience with cart management.

### Donation & Charitable Giving
- **Multi-Organization Support:** Donate to verified cultural, humanitarian, and community initiatives including the Apeksha Hospital (National Cancer Hospital).  
- **Flexible Donation Amounts:** Support causes with custom donation amounts in LKR.  
- **Secure Payment Processing:** Integrated payment gateway with comprehensive donation tracking.  
- **Transparent Fund Management:** Real-time donation statistics and fund allocation visibility.

### Administrative Dashboard
- **Comprehensive Analytics:** Track users (11 total, 11 active), suppliers (4), organizations (3), products (11), donations (7), and revenue (LKR 57,200.00 total, LKR 44,000.00 in donations).  
- **User Management:** Manage admin, customer, supplier, and organization accounts with role-based access control.  
- **Product Request Review:** Supplier product submission approval workflow.  
- **Donation Request Management:** Review and approve organizational donation requests.  
- **System Status Monitoring:** Real-time system health and database connectivity tracking.

### User Authentication & Profiles
- **Secure Login System:** Email/password authentication with "Remember me" functionality.  
- **Role-Based Access:** Distinct interfaces for Admin, Customer, Supplier, and Organization roles.  
- **Personalized Dashboards:** Customized views based on user type and permissions.  
- **Password Recovery:** Secure password reset functionality.

## Unique Value Proposition
Festora bridges the gap between traditional Sri Lankan culture and modern digital accessibility, offering:

- **Cultural Preservation:** Documenting and sharing festival traditions for future generations.  
- **Community Connection:** Connecting diaspora communities with their cultural roots.  
- **Economic Empowerment:** Supporting local artisans and cultural product suppliers.  
- **Social Impact:** Facilitating charitable giving to verified humanitarian causes.  
- **Educational Resource:** Comprehensive information about Sri Lankan religious and cultural practices.
